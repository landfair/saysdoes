Welcome to Says/Does Puzzle Generator!

What is Says/Does Analysis?

Says/Does is a powerful rhetorical analysis technique that helps students understand both the content (what the text says) and the function (what the text does) of each paragraph in an argument.

How This Tool Works:

1
Paste Your Text

Add an op-ed, essay, or argumentative text and click "Parse Paragraphs" to automatically split it.

2
Add Analysis

For each paragraph, write what it SAYS (summary with key terms) and what it DOES (rhetorical function).

3
Generate & Print

Create student handouts with shuffled cut-out cards and an answer key for instructors.

4
Save to Bank

Store your puzzles in the Puzzle Bank for easy access and reuse later.

💡 Teaching Tip

Students match the shuffled cards to the correct paragraphs, helping them practice close reading and understand how arguments are constructed at both the content and structural levels.
